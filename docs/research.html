01 · THE RESEARCH ARC

One body of work, presented through three papers.

The conference papers introduce the design tension and the security mechanism. The TCAD paper brings the measurements, attacks, and cross-technology analysis into one unified framework.

01

ICCAD 2024

SECURITY MECHANISM

On the Security Vulnerabilities of MRAM-based In-Memory Computing Architectures

Develops a statistical model-extraction framework, constructs basis-vector, least-squares, and SGD attacks, and validates them with measured outputs from the MRAM prototype.

  • Three query-based attacks
  • Bank- and network-level efficacy metrics
  • Real-chip model parameter recovery

02

IEDM 2024

DESIGN-SPACE VIEW

The Energy-Accuracy-Security Trade-off in Resistive In-memory Architectures

Introduces the three-way design problem and experimentally characterizes how operating conditions move energy, compute fidelity, and resilience to model extraction on a 22 nm MRAM IMC.

  • First experimental EAS characterization
  • Measured MRAM operating conditions
  • Path toward joint optimization

03

IEEE TCAD 2026

JOURNAL PAPER

Comprehending the Energy-Accuracy-Security Trade-offs in Embedded Non-Volatile IMCs

Unifies the statistical attacks and silicon measurements, then extends the EAS analysis through a validated behavioral model spanning MRAM, ReRAM, and FeFET technologies.

  • Measured and modeled EAS trade-offs
  • Cross-technology vulnerability analysis
  • Algorithmic paths toward better operating points
